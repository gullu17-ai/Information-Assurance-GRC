Purpose and expected output
Purpose: independently assess whether the audited group's Week 1–7 security and assurance design is aligned to business
objectives, defined requirements, risk appetite, selected frameworks, documented policies/processes, control design,
evidence, risk treatment, and financial, business outcomes
Required outputs:
(1) completed weighted checklist
(2) evidence register
(3) risk register and calculations
(4) control test records
(5) findings with corrective actions
(6) budget/business impact analysis
(7) one page executive/BOG dashboard



Important audit rules
      Audit what the group has documented and can evidence. Do not award full marks for a verbal claim alone
      Use the same criteria and scoring scale for every audited group; tailor the examples to the group's chosen industry
      Record evidence references, not just conclusions. Evidence can include diagrams, policy excerpts, risk registers,
       screenshots/mock evidence, control matrices, calculations, meeting notes, and test results

Rating discipline: a policy draft may score 1–2; a formally approved policy with owner and review cycle may score 3; evidence
of communication, operation, review, and tracked exceptions may support 4. A control design is not proof of operating
effectiveness.



Weighted score method
For each audit head, calculate the average rating of its applicable checklist items. Head points = Head weight × (average
rating ÷ 4). Total audit percentage = sum of earned head points ÷ sum of applicable head weights × 100. If no criteria are
N/A, the applicable weights total 100%.
